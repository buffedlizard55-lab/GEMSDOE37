Research question

Can geological persistence improve targeting?

We test whether stable, multiscale ridge structure adds value over a matched-budget single-scale prospectivity map—not whether a visually compelling map can tell a story.

01 · Geology

Five distinct hypotheses

Structure, magnetic/radiometric association, geophysical edges, a public fault catalogue, and a topological stability combination are ranked before evaluation.

Read the ranking →

02 · Stability

Persistence across scale

H0 superlevel persistence checks whether ridge-like highs survive 100, 200, and 400 m smoothing scales. Agreement is an explicit heuristic, not formal multiparameter persistence.

How we work

Evidence before confidence

What this project will do

  • Use official challenge rules and disclose what data came from unauthenticated mirrors.
  • Preserve a fixed hypothesis, detector, and promotion gate before looking at raster outcomes.
  • Compare candidates against the public catalogue under spatial separation and an equal pixel budget.
  • Make the TIFF fail closed on any output-format or promotion-gate failure.

What it will not claim

  • That public catalogue associations reproduce expert fault labels or hidden scoring.
  • That persistent topographic/geophysical edges prove mineralization.
  • That an unverified historical submission is an organizer-confirmed score.
  • That a good pseudo-holdout result guarantees a top leaderboard position.
