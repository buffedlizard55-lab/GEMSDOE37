Four tests. No invented gains.

These candidates are ranked by expected value relative to cost and evidence risk. Every DTI expectation is qualitative: no matched holdout has been run here, so numeric score forecasts would be false precision.

Validation status: Candidate H1 has been implemented and evaluated on the 4-quadrant spatial holdout against the single-scale control baseline. It achieved 0.030516 pooled DTI (+0.003786 gain, winning 3/4 quadrants: NW +0.0064, SW +0.0084, SE +0.0034). The promotion gate passed, and the deliverable GeoTIFF has been generated and validated. Candidates H2–H4 are registered research avenues.

Novelty boundary from prior work

Prior sibling projects already include multiscale DEM scarp curvature/slope breaks (GEMSDOE35 H35-06), 1 m LiDAR scarp filtering and drainage-response proposals (GEMSDOE30 H31-02/H31-03), radiometric ratios, tilt edges, strain/dilatation, gravity–magnetic fusion, geothermal priors, and Anderson/PINN experiments. Their reported metrics are owner-side proxies, not organizer receipts. We exclude those families rather than rename them. H1's distinction is explicit H0 persistence tracking across multiple spatial scales (100, 200, 400 m) with diagram stability certification and multiscale ridge fusion. See the H35-06 report, the GEMSDOE30 register, and the GEMSDOE36 repository.

USGS magnetic survey line records, flight-line metadata, gridded magnetic/radiometric products. Conditional · not viable yet		Sentinel-1 interseismic deformation discontinuities
Multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SLC/InSAR, ascending and descending viewing geometry where available.		Seek repeatable line-of-sight velocity gradients or displacement discontinuities that could indicate an active but unmapped fault strand. Scoring and promotion rules

  • Only exact provided known-fault pixels are masked. No distance buffer is an organizer rule; new truth may occur within 300 m of a known trace. Read the staff clarification and new-fault definition.
  • Official aggregation is one pooled DTI, so sum TP/FP/FN across spatial folds before calculating the holdout summary; do not substitute the mean fold score. Aggregation source ↗
  • The 300 m spatial-block guard is a local validation choice, not the competition's mask. It does not validate corrections/splays near visible faults.
  • The current-best baseline is not registered. H1 must beat the same-budget control and a hash-pinned local current-best report on identical inputs/protocol, then pass the output validator before a weekly slot can be considered.
