Four ways to find the line that is missing.

These are distinct, physically motivated hypotheses, ordered by expected upside relative to cost and evidence risk. The ordering is a research judgment—not a fabricated DTI forecast.

Evidence status: the H1 parameters were registered before evaluating any competition raster. No inputs, holdout, or organizer score are currently available. H1 is selected for implementation, not declared best.

Hypotheses, mechanisms & tests

Expected DTI gain is described qualitatively because the mirrored training rasters are not present and the official data tab requires sign-in. Each arm must be checked against the same public-catalogue spatial folds and prediction budget before a slot is considered.

Why H1 leads

It attacks the artifact problem directly

Ridges that disappear after modest smoothing are less compelling than responses that remain coherent over scales. This is a reason to test H1 first—not evidence that it will win. Its detector, thresholds, minimum feature-family support, comparator, and gate are fixed in the preregistration.

Ranking rationale

  1. H1 topological persistence: highest expected upside because it explicitly tests whether ridge evidence survives multiple smoothing scales and combines independent physical families; highest compute burden and unmeasured performance.
  2. H2 radiometric alteration ratios: attractive cost/upside if the official or free USGS coverage is complete; ratios could be confounded by bedrock, cover and flight-line effects.
  3. H3 strain orientation: locally available in principle and moderate cost; the geodetic-to-fault-strike relation is non-unique and any interaction must be spatially stable.
  4. H4 focal mechanisms: physically independent kinematic evidence, but completeness and spatial coverage may be too sparse for a fair regional raster.

Ranks 2–4 have no measured DTI gain. The qualitative terms “moderate” or “low-to-moderate” are prioritization labels, not numeric score estimates.

Scope: current code implements and preregisters H1 only. It does not claim these four methods were independently tested or compared in this checkout. All catalogue-based validation measures recovery of known/public lines, not hidden expert-only fault discovery.
