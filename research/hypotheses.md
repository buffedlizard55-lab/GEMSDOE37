## Ranking judgment

1. **H1 topological persistence** is selected for implementation because it directly tests the requested single-scale-artifact concern, uses only competition feature families, and yields interpretable birth/death and conditional stability margins. It is not assumed to win; computation and interpretation risks are significant.
2. **H2 radiometric alteration** has the most attractive cost/upside balance if the USGS GeoDAWN raster coverage is complete and the ratios survive cover/lithology confounding.
3. **H3 strain orientation** reuses data but its relation to surface fault strike is non-unique; a positive result must be stable by region and not reflect broad tectonic trends.
4. **H4 moment tensors** are physically useful independent evidence, but their spatial coverage and completeness at local magnitudes are unknown.

## Holdout design and promotion rule

- **Truth and leakage:** the only in-repository labels are expected to be the public USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue raster. A holdout on it measures recovery of withheld *catalogued* lines, not discovery of expert-only faults. Name it a **catalogue pseudo-holdout** throughout.
- **Blocks:** four preregistered spatial blocks (NW, NE, SW, SE) with a 300 m guard around evaluation boundaries. The transformations are unsupervised; any fitted calibration, band selection, or catalogue suppression must use training blocks only within each fold.
- **Matched budget:** compare the persistence candidate, a single-scale transform control, and the current local reference candidate at the same number of positive pixels and on the same valid grid. Use the official distance-weighted Tversky implementation in this repository; test its worked example against the competition's rounded worked example before use.
- **Promotion gate:** candidate mean paired DTI must exceed the current holdout best by a preregistered practical margin and improve at least three of four folds, with no fold collapse beyond a preregistered tolerance. Store all fold values, label and feature hashes, candidate budget, parameter file, and script version.
- **Slot status:** **no slot is spent unless this gate passes and the submission TIFF passes the byte-level format validator**. A catalogue pseudo-holdout pass is necessary but not sufficient to claim private-set or final-round improvement.
- **Current state:** no input raster or holdout is present in this checkout. Therefore H1 has not been run and has not beaten the current holdout best. Do not emit or label any H1 artifact as slot-eligible until the data and baseline are verified.
