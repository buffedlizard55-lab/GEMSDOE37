## H33-2-B2: what is reported versus what is supported

The [GEMSDOE32 owner site](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html) and [owner hypothesis report](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/hypotheses.html) describe H33-2-B2 as a **removal-only** change from a user-reported 0.2708 base. The owner report gives 37,654 positive pixels, a four-fold proxy gain of +0.004870, and a projected 0.2747; it explicitly says no organizer score existed for the artifacts when that report was published. The user-supplied history later associates the H33 filename with 0.2778. The official board does not identify submitted TIFF filenames, so it cannot establish artifact-level attribution. These records differ in source and timing; the H33 score association remains **unverified**, not disproven.

### The scientifically supportable explanation

Sparse ranking or thinning can improve DTI when it removes predictions whose marginal contribution to nearby truth is smaller than their false-positive mass. In the published metric, a prediction farther than 300 m from any truth earns no TP credit but still contributes FP; near an unmatched truth it may earn triangular-kernel credit. Thus a removal-only operation can help a particular proxy if it removes low-credit mass while preserving useful coverage. That is a plausible explanation of the **owner-reported proxy**, not a causal explanation of an organizer score.

**The earlier catalogue-mask explanation is incorrect.** DrivenData staff clarify that the known-fault mask excludes the provided known-fault pixels **exactly**; there is no buffer around traces, and predictions on nearby unmasked pixels are scored normally. New/corrected/continued/splayed truth can occur within 300 m of a known trace. Therefore the H33 `B=2` operation's suppression of a 200 m neighbourhood is not an organizer-required mask: the exact on-trace pixels are neutral under the mask, while the surrounding 1–2 pixel cells may either earn credit or incur penalty depending on hidden truth. The old claim that all `B=2` points “earned zero TP and only added penalty” does not follow from the rules.

Sources: [staff scoring clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4), [new-fault definition](https://community.drivendata.org/t/where-do-you-draw-the-line/11536/2), [pooled leaderboard aggregation](https://community.drivendata.org/t/leaderboard-aggregation-pooled-over-public-test-pixels-or-mean-of-per-chunk-scores/11550/2).

## What cannot be inferred

- The method behind the official public top entries cannot be recovered from participant names and scores alone.
- A user/team-reported artifact value is not an organizer score without an artifact-specific receipt.
- The 4-fold owner proxy is not the organizer's pooled public/private score and is not expert-only truth validation.
- A positive score association does not prove that B=2 suppression, Andersonian constraints, any external prior, or any particular transform caused the result.
- A persistent H0 component is a connected-component maximum in a scalar response field; it is not by itself an elongated geological line, mapped fault, or geothermal conduit.
