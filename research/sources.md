## Irregularities and blockers

1. **Top-score mismatch:** user says 0.3195 is the current top; official public leaderboard at the dated check showed 0.3262 first, 0.3222 second, and 0.3195 third.
2. **H33 score/file attribution:** user associates one H33 filename with 0.2778. The public leaderboard does not expose filenames; the GEMSDOE32 owner page says its H33 artifacts had no organizer score at its publication time and shows a projected estimate. Attribution is unresolved.
3. **Initial checkout empty:** on opening this repository, only an 11-byte README was tracked. There were no `scripts/download_competition_data.sh`, `scripts/prepare_data.py`, data directory contents, tests, site, or prior GEMSDOE37 holdout results. Those cannot be assumed to exist from a prompt referring to earlier sessions.
4. **Official data access:** the DrivenData data tab redirects to login; the official participant files could not be fetched without a competition account. The task's Dropbox URLs are mirrors and currently were not downloaded into this checkout.
5. **Network asymmetry:** the public page-fetch tool could retrieve DrivenData and source pages, and `git` could reach GitHub, but shell `curl` returned TLS connection errors for DrivenData/Dropbox. A GitHub-hosted runner may have different access; this has not yet been proven.
6. **No result/no TIFF:** without validated inputs and a reproducible spatial holdout, no geological candidate, score improvement, or submission-ready TIFF can honestly be claimed. This is a blocking item, not a hidden success.
7. **Public leaderboard is not final evaluation:** the rules describe private initial-round labels and later expert-updated labels. A public score does not establish final-round performance or award eligibility.
8. **AI disclosure and eligibility:** official rules require AI-use disclosure and apply eligibility conditions. This engineering repo cannot certify the registered competitor or author the competitor's legal attestation.
